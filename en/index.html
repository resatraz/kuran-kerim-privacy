Privacy Policy

Türkçe sürüm: Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The Kur'an-ı Kerim application ("the App"), developed by Peyam Raz, takes the privacy and data security of its users very seriously. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App.

Data We Collect and Permissions

Our App does not collect, store, or share your personal data (name, email address, phone number, location, etc.) in any way. The App only requests the following permission to provide its core functionality:

  • Notification Permission: Used to send daily verse reminders and reading notifications. This permission is processed entirely on your device; no personal data is transmitted to any remote server.

Third-Party Services and APIs

The App may connect to external API services to download/stream audio files and to display Qur'an text. Other than the technical data required for the App to operate reliably, these services do not collect any personal data or identity information of yours.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Used to send crash and stability reports. Only diagnostic information from the moment of a crash (device model, operating system version, crash log) is transmitted; no name, email address, location, or other personal data is transmitted. It creates no account and collects no username or password.

App Store Ratings: Apple's in-app rating system only affects the App's store ranking; it collects no personal data.

Data Deletion Requests

The App has no user accounts, so Apple's account deletion requirement does not apply. All data stored on your device (bookmarks, memorization records, settings, downloaded audio) is permanently removed when you delete the App. Crashlytics crash reports are anonymous and contain no personal data, so they cannot be deleted individually; if you still have a request, feel free to contact us.

Data Security

The App does not ask you to create a user account, does not require you to sign in, and does not request any sensitive data entry. Therefore, there is no risk of any data breach.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general application intended for religious education, recitation, and reading, and is suitable for all age groups including children. No data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time in accordance with legal regulations or application updates. The current policy will always be available on this page.
